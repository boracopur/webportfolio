Add more dynamism to the website, but keep it subtle, premium, and restrained.

Important:
Do NOT turn this into a flashy Persona-style UI, playful motion design piece, or gimmicky animated landing page.
The motion should feel cinematic, technical, atmospheric, and controlled — like the interface has quiet life in it.

Motion direction:
- subtle, intentional, refined
- sharp and smooth, not bouncy
- responsive but never loud
- slightly futuristic / technical
- elegant and confident, not playful

Add restrained motion in these ways:

1. Hero entrance
- Use a clean staged reveal for the hero content
- headline, subtext, and CTA can enter with slight delay differences
- use fade + slight translate
- no bounce, no overshoot, no exaggerated scale
- keep the entrance smooth and confident

2. Scroll-based atmosphere
- Add very subtle scroll response to the hero/background
- slight parallax or depth shift is okay
- the grid/background can move or fade very slightly with scroll
- keep it almost unnoticeable
- avoid obvious “parallax showcase” behavior

3. Section reveals
- As sections enter viewport, reveal them with restrained motion
- use soft fade, slight upward or lateral movement
- stagger children very lightly where appropriate
- keep durations short and polished
- avoid dramatic slide-ins

4. Project card interaction
- Add subtle hover response to featured work and project cards
- slight image shift, slight scale, or controlled overlay transition is fine
- keep hover premium and sharp
- no aggressive zoom, no flashy glow, no game-menu effects

5. Button interaction
- Buttons should feel alive and responsive
- use subtle hover transitions, color shifts, border transitions, or tiny movement
- keep them crisp and high-end
- avoid chunky, playful, or exaggerated hover effects

6. Divider / line behavior
- If lines or separators exist, allow some of them to reveal on scroll or animate in quietly
- motion should feel architectural and technical
- no unnecessary decorative animation loops

7. Footer / final section
- Give the closing section a calm reveal
- make it feel intentional and complete
- avoid turning it into a second flashy animated hero

Motion rules:
- no bouncing
- no springy cartoon feel
- no stickman, mascots, or playful objects
- no overuse of glow effects
- no excessive looping animations
- no constant movement that distracts from the artwork
- no heavy Persona-inspired UI gimmicks

Overall goal:
The site should feel more alive, more polished, and more cinematic through motion — but still serious, portfolio-first, and environment-artist-focused.
Motion should support the atmosphere, not become the main feature.
