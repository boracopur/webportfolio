Refine the portfolio visually again, but this time address an important overall concern:

The site currently looks strong, premium, and professional, but it is starting to feel a bit too severe, too rigid, and slightly too cold. I do NOT want to lose the dark cinematic identity, but I want the site to feel a little more human, atmospheric, and artist-driven — not just hard, strict, and hyper-controlled.

Important clarification:
Do not redesign the site from scratch.
Do not make it playful, childish, colorful, soft-corporate, rounded, or generic.
Do not turn it into a bubbly friendly portfolio.
Do not remove the premium dark mood.
The goal is NOT to soften it into something weak.
The goal is to keep it premium and serious, while reducing the overly harsh / overly mechanical feeling.

Overall direction:
Move the visual tone slightly from:
- severe
- rigid
- ultra-controlled
- agency-like
- cold technical

toward:
- atmospheric
- human
- artistic
- premium
- confident
- cinematic
- quietly expressive

Key concern to solve:
Right now the site can feel a bit too “commanding” and not enough “alive”.
It should feel like the portfolio of an environment artist with taste, atmosphere, and sensitivity — not only discipline and structure.

VISUAL CHANGES TO EXPLORE

1. Reduce the overall harshness slightly
- Keep the dark palette and strong contrast
- But reduce the “hard black + hard white + hard red everywhere” feeling
- Make the site feel a little more lived-in and less mechanically sharp

2. Color tuning
- Keep the red accent, but make it slightly richer, warmer, and more elegant
- Avoid anything too neon or too aggressive
- Let the red feel like a signature accent, not like an alarm color
- Consider using a slightly softer off-white instead of pure harsh white in some areas

3. Typography mood
- Keep the strong display font in major places
- But reduce the sense that every important element is shouting
- Let some headings, labels, and UI text breathe more naturally
- Maintain confidence, but reduce the “graphic brand system” feeling
- Keep it sharp, but slightly more editorial / atmospheric

4. UI tone
- Buttons, cards, tags, and section dividers should feel premium and restrained
- Reduce any remaining “boxed component” or “template UI” energy
- Make the interface feel less like a site builder and more like a designed portfolio
- Keep structure, but remove any visual stiffness that feels too synthetic

5. Spacing and rhythm
- Keep generous negative space
- But make the page feel less sterile
- Improve rhythm so sections feel more naturally paced and less mechanically spaced
- Let the composition breathe in a more intentional, artistic way

6. Section atmosphere
- Preserve the portfolio-first approach
- But allow sections to feel a little more alive and a little less diagrammatic
- Works should still be the strongest section
- About / Experience / Footer should feel more integrated into the same emotional world, not just structured blocks

7. Motion / interaction tone
- Keep motion subtle and premium
- Avoid flashy or gimmicky animation
- But make the reveals feel smoother, more organic, and less like delayed loading
- Hover states should feel elegant and atmospheric, not abrupt or overly UI-driven

8. Personality balance
- The site should still feel serious
- But not so serious that it becomes distant or impersonal
- A little bit of personal character is welcome, as long as it feels tasteful and integrated
- Keep the portfolio mature, but not emotionally flat

What to avoid:
- playful design
- cartoon energy
- over-rounding
- colorful gradients everywhere
- startup aesthetics
- fake “friendly” redesign
- excessive animation
- overly decorative flourishes
- removing the site’s core dark identity

Design goal:
The final result should feel like a strong, premium, cinematic environment artist portfolio that still has humanity, atmosphere, and artistic warmth inside it.
It should feel less harsh and less rigid, without becoming soft or generic.

In short:
Keep the portfolio dark, premium, and confident — but make it feel a little more human, a little more atmospheric, and a little less severe.
