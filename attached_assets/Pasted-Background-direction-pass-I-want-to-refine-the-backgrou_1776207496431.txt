Background direction pass

I want to refine the background without redesigning the site and without losing the current premium dark identity.

Current feeling:
The grid pattern works as a technical structure, but the background still reads a bit too much like a graphic surface. I want it to feel deeper, more atmospheric, and more spatial — like it belongs to an environment artist portfolio, not just a clean UI system.

Main direction:
Keep the grid as a base layer, but transform the background from a flat design backdrop into a cinematic spatial atmosphere.

What I want:

* preserve the dark, premium, controlled mood
* keep the grid, but push it further into the background
* make the background feel more alive through depth, tonal variation, and atmospheric layering
* add a subtle sense of space / volume / invisible architecture behind the content
* make the darkness feel like a real environment, not just a black surface

Visual approach:

* use the grid as the structural layer
* add very subtle large-scale tonal gradients or atmospheric haze above or behind it
* think in terms of cinematic depth, soft shadow masses, and spatial atmosphere
* allow the background to have slightly different density or tonal presence across the page
* make it feel like soft light and shadow are shaping the space very faintly

Important:
Do NOT add obvious visual objects.
Do NOT add decorative blobs.
Do NOT add flashy particles.
Do NOT add strong cyberpunk effects.
Do NOT add colorful gradient washes.
Do NOT make it look like a modern startup landing page.

Avoid:

* obvious motion-heavy background elements
* aurora-like effects
* visible floating particles
* excessive noise texture
* bright accent glows
* anything that competes with the portfolio work

Desired feeling:

* dark but not flat
* structured but not sterile
* atmospheric but not decorative
* cinematic but restrained
* spatial, not graphic
* alive, but quiet

Best direction to explore:
A layered background with:

1. a very dark charcoal/near-black base
2. a subtle faded grid pattern
3. a soft atmospheric haze / tonal veil
4. very faint large shadow planes or architectural light falloff
5. optional minimal filmic texture only if it remains almost invisible

The result should feel like the site exists inside a dark cinematic volume, not on top of a flat black page.

Do not redesign the page sections.
Do not change the site’s identity.
This is a background atmosphere pass only.
