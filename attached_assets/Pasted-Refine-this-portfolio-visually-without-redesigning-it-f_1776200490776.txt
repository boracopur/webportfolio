Refine this portfolio visually without redesigning it from scratch.

Overall direction:
This should feel like the portfolio of a serious 3D Environment Artist, not a creative agency or generic AI landing page. Keep the current dark, cinematic, industrial, technical mood. The design should support the work, not overpower it.

Keep:
- black / red / white palette
- subtle grid background
- bold premium tone
- dark, minimal, structured visual language
- strong featured works section

Improve:
- typography hierarchy
- spacing rhythm
- layout balance on wide screens
- button styling
- section composition
- visual restraint
- portfolio-first presentation

Typography:
- Keep Monument only for major display use such as hero emphasis and section titles
- Reduce Monument usage in nav, buttons, labels, metadata, and smaller UI
- Make job titles and smaller headings less loud
- Create clearer hierarchy between section titles, card titles, role titles, and body text
- Use smaller UI text more functionally and less decoratively

Color:
- Keep the red accent but make it slightly deeper and more premium
- Reduce how often the same red emphasis appears
- Make accent color usage feel more selective and intentional

Background:
- Keep the grid but make it more subtle
- Push it further back with lower opacity
- Reduce or remove unnecessary decorative particles/noise

Spacing and layout:
- Improve vertical rhythm across sections
- Make empty space feel intentional, not unfinished
- Tighten internal spacing inside content blocks
- Keep generous section spacing but balance it better
- Improve right-side visual balance in sections that currently feel too left-heavy

Hero:
- Keep it bold and minimal
- Do not redesign it completely
- Improve composition so the left side does not feel too compressed and the right side does not feel too empty
- Preserve strong first impression but reduce the feeling that typography alone is carrying the section

Works section:
- Keep this as a major visual anchor
- Make the design system support the artwork rather than compete with it
- Improve spacing and hierarchy around featured work and the project grid

Experience / abilities / toolset:
- Reduce the template-like component feel
- Simplify framing and decorative styling
- Make this area feel cleaner, calmer, and more professional

Buttons and CTA:
- Reduce bulky border/shadow/sticker feel
- Keep buttons sharp, minimal, and premium
- Differentiate primary and secondary CTA more clearly

Footer:
- Make it feel less like a second hero
- Keep it bold but calmer and more final
- Create a more mature closing section

Avoid:
- creative agency feel
- fashion branding feel
- generic AI template feel
- overdesigned UI
- excessive repetition of the same visual motif
- loud styling in every section

Do not:
- rewrite the content
- remove the dark cinematic identity
- make it corporate
- make it generic
- overcomplicate the layout
- rebuild the whole page
